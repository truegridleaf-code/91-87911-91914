About Our Company

60+ Years of Quality Net Manufacturing on Suraj Kund Road, Meerut

Established in 1965 at Meerut (Uttar Pradesh, India), “Meerut Net Centre” (brand NETCO POWER®), is a premier Sole Proprietorship Firm engaged in manufacturing, wholesaling, and supplying a comprehensive assortment of nets.
